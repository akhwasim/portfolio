about

I'm a Computer Science student from Bengaluru, India, with a strong interest in systems programming, developer tools, and artificial intelligence. I enjoy understanding how software works beneath the surface and building tools that solve practical problems.

I recently built Luna, an AI-powered terminal assistant, and Compass a platform that helps developers discover open source issues they can realistically contribute to. Both projects reinforced my interest in designing software that combines deterministic systems with AI to create tools that are reliable, explainable, and genuinely useful.

Beyond individual projects, I'm interested in developer tooling, adaptive computing, and context-aware AI systems. I enjoy exploring how software can assist people without sacrificing transparency or user control. I'm currently seeking internship and research opportunities where I can continue building systems software and contribute to challenging engineering problems.
